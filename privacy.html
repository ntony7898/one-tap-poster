Privacy Policy

One-Tap Poster (test build) · Effective October 26, 2026 · Developer: Tony Nguyen (GitHub: ntony7898)

Draft stub for the test build — to be finalised before an App Store release or before a backend is connected.

What the test build stores

  • Your handle, display name, bio, posters (as text + style + seed), likes, follows and daily-challenge entries are saved in your browser’s local storage on this device only. Clearing site data or using “Reset on-device data” removes them.
  • Posters are rendered on your device. We do not upload images or text.

Sharing

When you tap Share, your device’s share sheet sends the PNG to the app you choose (e.g. TikTok, Instagram); that app’s privacy policy then applies. “Copy link” creates a URL that contains your poster text, so anyone with the link can see it.

Demo community

Other creators shown in the test build are fictional and generated on your device.

No tracking

No analytics, ads, cookies or third-party requests. Fonts are bundled with the app.

Future versions

If accounts and a server are added, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected (e.g. account email, posts, reports), why, how long it is kept, and how to delete your account in-app.
